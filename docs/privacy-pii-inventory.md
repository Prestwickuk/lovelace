# LOVELACE Booking System — Personal Data Inventory & Lifecycle Controls

> **Scope & Accuracy Notice:** This document inventories personal information stored across PostgreSQL tables in the LOVELACE Booking System as of Phase 4 Live OTA & Channel Distribution (`migrations/0001_init.sql` through `migrations/0019_phase4_live_ota_sync.sql`). It is an engineering and operational privacy inventory; it does **not** certify UK GDPR compliance or legal compliance on its own, because legal retention periods, statutory tax/accounting exemptions, third-party redaction decisions, and data controller privacy notices require operator and legal determination.

### Final acceptance & Phase 4 audit snapshot contract (`0018`–`0019`)

Replacement JSONB is validated by resource type derived from the target row in `audit_logs`. The 41 supported types are: `admin_session`, `admin_user`, `audit_log`, `availability_block`, `availability_release_rule`, `blockout_group`, `booking_channel`, `booking_hold`, `channel_connection`, `channel_external_reservation`, `channel_listing_mapping`, `channel_sync_job`, `channel_webhook_event`, `communication_automation_rule`, `communication_template`, `confirmation_access_token`, `email_outbox`, `extra`, `guest`, `guest_communication`, `guest_enquiry`, `guest_note`, `guest_preference`, `housekeeping_task`, `maintenance_asset`, `maintenance_attachment`, `maintenance_category`, `maintenance_contractor`, `maintenance_location`, `maintenance_request`, `maintenance_schedule`, `payment_exception`, `payment_webhook_event`, `property`, `property_booking_policy`, `property_retention_policy`, `rate_plan`, `rate_rule`, `reservation`, `unit`, and `unit_type`.

The exact per-resource key allowlists and each key's type are defined in Go `auditResourceAllowlists` in `internal/service/audit_sanitizer.go` and mirrored by PostgreSQL `audit_resource_key_rule` in migration `0018`. Those maps are the complete accepted-field contract: identifiers are UUIDs (or explicitly nullable UUIDs); timestamps, dates, and local times use their defined formats; status and category fields use fixed enums; references and operational codes use bounded non-free-text tokens; flags are booleans; counts, nights, and monetary totals are bounded integers; UUID arrays and the few approved nested structures have their own key and type rules. For example, a guest snapshot can retain `guest_id`, `status`, `has_email`, `has_phone`, `changed_fields`, and `version`; a reservation can retain `reservation_id`, `reference`, `status`, `nights`, `total_pence`, and `unit_ids`. A nested `blockout_group.group` object has its own explicit field map. The Go and SQL allowlists are tested together for every supported resource type.

Unknown top-level or nested keys, unsupported resource types, arbitrary free text, wrong JSON types, invalid enum values, email addresses, UK or international phone patterns, phone-like numbers in numeric fields, control characters, strings over 128 characters, arrays over 64 items, objects over 40 keys, and nesting deeper than four levels are rejected with SQLSTATE `22023`. Error messages do not echo submitted values. Go sanitization strips fields outside the same resource-specific allowlist before submitting a replacement; PostgreSQL repeats validation at the approved function and table-trigger boundaries, so calling the function directly as `lovelace_app` does not bypass it.

The `audit_logs` source is append-only. `audit_log_redactions` stores a separate append-only overlay, and `audit_logs_effective` presents the permitted replacement snapshots plus masked actor email, IP address, and user agent. Raw historical audit rows remain unchanged for integrity and are not returned through normal application reads.

## 2. Audit Trail vs. Privacy & Historical Redaction Architecture

1. **PII Minimisation Going Forward (`internal/service/audit_sanitizer.go`):**
   - `Service.recordAudit` enforces two layers:
     1. Explicit minimized snapshot builders (`auditGuestSnapshot`, `auditReservationSnapshot`, `auditBookingHoldSnapshot`, `auditEnquirySnapshot`, `auditEnquiryMessageSnapshot`, `auditCommunicationSnapshot`, `auditMergeEventSnapshot`, `auditPreferenceSnapshot`, `auditNoteSnapshot`, `auditConsentSnapshot`, `auditAdminUserSnapshot`) recording IDs, references, lifecycle statuses, versions, changed field names, and financial totals without raw guest contact fields or free-text bodies.
     2. Defense-in-depth payload scrubbing (`sanitizeAuditPayload`) stripping credential keys (`password`, `password_hash`, `secret`, `token_hash`, `csrf_token`, `customer_session_hash`, `api_key`, etc.) and raw personal data fields on PII-bearing resource types.
   - Public-facing hold/cancellation events record `"guest@public.lovelace"` as `actor_email` instead of raw customer email addresses.
2. **Append-Only Integrity & Controlled Historical Redaction (`migrations/0013`–`0018`):**
   - `audit_logs` retains `trg_audit_logs_immutable` (`BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE`) and `trg_audit_logs_no_truncate` unconditionally — raw historical `audit_logs` rows remain immutable for audit tamper-evidence and are never modified in place.
   - Historical rows requiring privacy masking use the append-only overlay table `audit_log_redactions` (`redacted_before_state`, `redacted_after_state`, `redacted_metadata`, `reason`, `reason_category`, `redacted_by`, `redacted_at`) written exclusively through `SECURITY DEFINER` `redact_audit_log_entry(...)` via `Service.RedactHistoricalAuditLog` (`POST /api/v1/properties/{propertyID}/audit-logs/{auditLogID}/redact`), gated strictly by both `PermGuestsAnonymize` and `PermAuditRead`, active `owner_admin`/`manager` with `must_rotate_password = FALSE`, and property scope, and generating an immutable `audit_log.redacted` provenance entry.
   - Both PostgreSQL (`validate_audit_redaction_snapshot` + `audit_resource_key_rule` + `validate_non_pii_reason` inside `redact_audit_log_entry` and `trg_audit_log_redactions_non_pii` `BEFORE INSERT`, deriving `resource_type` authoritatively from `audit_logs`) and Go (`auditResourceAllowlists` in `audit_sanitizer.go` + `prepareAuditOverlaySnapshot` + `ValidateNonPIIText`) reject unrecognised resource types, unapproved keys, arbitrary free-text, phone-like JSON numbers or digit runs, wrong value types, excessive nesting/size, email addresses, international/UK phone numbers, and control characters (preserving only allowlisted structural IDs, enums, codes, timestamps, counts, permitted numeric totals, or `{"redacted": true}`).
   - Normal application and API reads (`ListAuditLogs` / `ListEffectiveAuditLogs` / `GetAuditLogByID`) query the `audit_logs_effective` view (`LEFT JOIN audit_log_redactions`), never exposing direct `lovelace_app` `SELECT` access on `audit_logs` or `audit_log_redactions`, while retaining redaction accountability metadata (`reason`, `reason_category`, `redacted_by`, `redacted_at`).

## 3. Operational Boundaries & Unimplemented Scope

- **Unlinked Anonymous Holds & Unlinked Enquiries:** Abandoned checkout `booking_holds` that never completed payment (`guest_id IS NULL`) and unlinked web/phone `guest_enquiries` (`guest_id IS NULL AND reservation_id IS NULL`) are **not** matched automatically by unvalidated email/phone strings during `ExportGuestData` or `AnonymizeGuest` to avoid cross-customer collision / impersonation risk.
- **Advisory Retention Policies Only (`ExecutionMode = "ADVISORY_ONLY"`):** `property_retention_policies` still stores advisory windows (`auto_anonymize_enabled` remains disabled and rejected if set `true`) because automated background deletion/anonymisation requires operator sign-off on legal retention rules in a dedicated future phase.
- **No Email Recall After SMTP/Provider Acceptance:** Once the mail transport accepts an outbound message, LOVELACE cannot recall it remotely; if concurrent `AnonymizeGuest` encounters a message already accepted by transport, `email_outbox.status` truthfully records `SENT` and `communication_delivery_events` records `ACCEPTED` while scrubbing stored recipient/body copies.
- **Milestone 4 Guest Portal Privacy Gates (`Section 7.2` Remains Open):** Phase 3 Milestone 4 (`0026_phase3_m4_guest_portal.sql`–`0027_phase3_m4_keyed_otp_hmac.sql`, disabled by default) allows authenticated Tier B guests with fresh (`<= 15m`) step-up verification to submit tracked, request-only personal data access (`SAR`) and erasure requests (`guest_enquiries` for staff review, returning a uniform receipt without disclosing prior request IDs/status). Automated customer data export (`ExportGuestData`), immediate self-service anonymisation (`AnonymizeGuest`), rights-request status disclosure, and portal IP/user-agent telemetry hash collection remain intentionally unimplemented until the owner resolves the Section 7.2 identity-assurance, statutory-retention, and telemetry-retention questions in [Phase 3 Milestone 4 Guest Portal](phase3-milestone4-guest-portal.md).

## 4. Governance & Legal Review Decisions Required Before Production Policy Sign-Off

1. Determine property-specific statutory retention durations for financial `reservations`, `payments`, `refunds`, and unlinked expired `booking_holds` / unlinked `guest_enquiries`.
2. Define operator SOP for third-party / exemption review before releasing `ExportGuestData` outputs in response to UK GDPR Art. 15 Subject Access Requests (`notes`, free-text `reason`, `description`).
3. Establish privileged governance approval workflow for invoking historical `RedactHistoricalAuditLog` vs. retaining security telemetry (`ip_address`, `user_agent`).
4. Confirm direct DB role hardening (`lovelace_app` DML-only) and physical backup retention / purge cadence so erased records expire from encrypted database backups on the operator's chosen schedule.

## 5. Phase 5 BI & Accounting Export Privacy Boundary

- **Zero Guest PII in BI Endpoints or Accounting Exports:** None of `/api/v1/properties/{propertyID}/reports/*`, `/reports`, or `lovelace.accounting.v1` accounting CSVs (`booking_line_items`, `payment_ledger`, `refund_ledger`) return or export `guest_name`, `guest_email`, `guest_phone`, `address_json`, `notes`, or `guest_id`.
- **Anonymised Stay Segregation:** Guest retention (`GET .../reports/retention`) follows canonical `merged_into_guest_id` merge lineage strictly to compute aggregate repeat counts; stays whose canonical guest is `ANONYMIZED` are isolated into an aggregate count (`anonymized_guest_stays_count`) without unmasking identity or linking erased records to named cohorts.
- **PII-Free Export Audit Trail (`report_export`):** `ExportAccountingReport` records only non-PII parameters (`id`, `property_id`, `export_type`, `format`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `currency`, `row_count`, `created_at`) enforced by PostgreSQL `0020_phase5_reporting_bi.sql`.
